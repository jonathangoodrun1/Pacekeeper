Music

No tracks — coach only

Why the app plays your music instead of Apple Music: iOS gives web apps no way to duck another app's audio — the moment this app speaks, Apple Music is interrupted and won't come back. Playing your own files here means the coach ducks the music properly and it lifts right back up. Files stay on your phone; nothing is uploaded.

Cadence

Three steps in, two steps out. The odd count means your exhale lands on alternating feet instead of hammering the same side every stride — the single best injury-prevention lever in a long run.
